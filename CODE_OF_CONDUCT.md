# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want AI Learning Lab to be a welcoming, harassment-free place to learn, whatever someone's experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to AI or to open source.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask questions before drawing conclusions.
- Respect people's time, privacy, and differing viewpoints.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualized language or attention.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially where possible. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict, or ban participants who violate this code.
